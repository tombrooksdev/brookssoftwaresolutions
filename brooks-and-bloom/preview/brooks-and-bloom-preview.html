Where do your sessions take place?

Most sessions are outdoors around Coton in the Elms and the National Forest, including woodland, meadows and parkland near Rosliston. Baby sessions usually happen in your own home. I am happy to come to a place that means something to you.

What happens if it rains?

We keep an eye on the forecast together. If the weather looks poor I will move your session to the next available date at no cost.

About

Hello, and welcome.

The photographer behind Brooks & Bloom. Natural, relaxed photography for families, little ones and pets, from a small village on the edge of the National Forest.

Your portrait
Behind the scenes

Moments worth remembering

Brooks & Bloom started with a simple idea: family photos should feel like your family, not like a photo shoot. The best pictures happen when everyone relaxes, so that is what my sessions are built around.

We might wander through the woods, chase the dog across a meadow or snuggle up at home with a brand new baby. I will guide you gently along the way, but there is no pressure to pose perfectly. Wriggles, giggles and muddy knees are all welcome.

I'm based in Coton in the Elms, a few minutes from Rosliston and the heart of the National Forest, and I photograph families across Burton upon Trent, Swadlincote, Lichfield and the villages in between.

Privacy policy

Your privacy matters

How Brooks & Bloom Photography collects, uses and looks after your personal information. Last updated November 2026.

Who I am

Brooks & Bloom Photography is a photography business based in Coton in the Elms, Derbyshire. I am the data controller for information you share through this website. You can contact me at hello@brooksandbloomphotography.co.uk.

What I collect

When you use the contact form I collect your name, email address, the session you are interested in and your message. When you book, Pixieset collects your booking and payment details on my behalf under their own privacy policy.

How I use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, arrange and deliver your session, and send your gallery. I never sell your details or add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Photographs

I will only share images from your session on my website or social media with your permission. You can change your mind at any time.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for 6 years for accounting purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you. Email me and I will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website uses only the cookies needed for it to work, such as protecting the contact form from spam. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated and you will be asked for consent.
